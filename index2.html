My Video Game Collection

Worlds Worth Getting Lost In.

Six games, six different experiences. From building entire worlds and exploring futuristic cities to surviving terrifying encounters and questioning the meaning of existence.

01 / SANDBOX

Minecraft

A limitless sandbox where creativity and adventure come together. Explore randomly generated worlds, build incredible structures, gather resources, and survive against the dangers of the night. Minecraft makes every adventure your own.

02 / PSYCHOLOGICAL

ALTER EGO

Created by Caramel Column, ALTER EGO is a psychological exploration game centered around self-discovery. Through conversations, introspection, and personality tests, you explore the depths of your identity and uncover a mysterious story.

03 / OPEN WORLD RPG

Cyberpunk 2077

Welcome to Night City, a futuristic metropolis filled with technology, corruption, and ambition. Play as V, a mercenary searching for immortality in a world where humanity and machinery are increasingly intertwined.

04 / SURVIVAL ADVENTURE

Subnautica

Stranded on an alien ocean planet, explore the depths, collect resources, craft equipment, and uncover secrets hidden beneath the waves. A beautiful yet terrifying underwater world awaits.

05 / CO-OP HORROR

Lethal Company

Work for the Company by scavenging abandoned moons for scrap while trying to meet your profit quota. Teamwork is essential, but dangerous creatures and unpredictable encounters can turn even the simplest expedition into complete chaos.

06 / SCI-FI HORROR

SOMA

Deep beneath the ocean, in an isolated research facility, something has gone terribly wrong. Explore an unsettling underwater world while uncovering a story about consciousness, identity, artificial intelligence, and what it truly means to be human.

A collection of six of my favorite video games.
